Under the Hood: 3-Phase P2P Radio

How Bumblebee establishes direct high-bandwidth links without routers or cellular infrastructure.

01

Phase 1: BLE Autonomous Discovery 2.4 GHz Ultra Low Power

Devices advertise a lightweight service UUID while scanning for neighboring phones. Radios consume minimal battery until a peer is spotted.

02

Phase 2: Mutual 4-Digit PIN Handshake Curve25519 ECDH

Both users review identical 4-digit verification digits on their screens to ensure no man-in-the-middle or spoofing before accepting.

03

Phase 3: High-Speed Wi-Fi Direct Nectar Tunnel Up to 250 Mbps Local Radio

Radio negotiates a direct ad-hoc Wi-Fi tunnel. Voice memos, HD photos, and delivery receipts stream directly between radios with sub-second latency.

The Story Behind Bumblebee

Communication is a human right.
Even when the grid goes dark.

In a world interconnected by centralized undersea cables and cell towers, a single hurricane, earthquake, power outage, or government shutdown can silence millions. Bumblebee was built to restore sovereignty to local communication.

01. Total Grid Resilience

When natural disasters flood cell stations or festivals overload networks, every smartphone running Bumblebee transforms into an ad-hoc local transmission node. No single point of failure can take the network down.

02. Uncompromised Privacy

Centralized messengers log your metadata, phone numbers, and IP addresses. Bumblebee creates an ephemeral air-gapped radio tunnel between devices. When communication never touches the cloud, surveillance is impossible.

03. 100% Free & Open Source

Licensed under the permissive MIT License. No venture paywalls, no recurring subscriptions, and zero ads. The entire codebase is transparent, auditable, and built for global community benefit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about off-grid mesh communication.

Does Bumblebee really work with zero internet?

Yes, 100%. Bumblebee communicates purely through physical radio waves (Bluetooth Low Energy and Wi-Fi Direct). No cell towers, SIM cards, Wi-Fi routers, or internet service providers are ever used.

What is the communication range and network topology?

Direct peer-to-peer radio range typically spans 30 to 100+ meters in open air or clear line-of-sight. Direct 1-to-1 P2P messaging, PTT voice memos, and HD photo transmission are fully production-tested and verified using Google Play Services Nearby Connections (P2P_CLUSTER). Multi-hop store-and-forward relay mesh is currently in active experimental development on our roadmap.

Why does it need Location permission on Android?

Google Android's security architecture mandates Location permission for apps performing Bluetooth Low Energy scanning and Wi-Fi Direct discovery. Bumblebee uses this solely for radio device discovery and never uploads your location to any server.

How does Bumblebee send high-resolution photos offline?

Standard Google Nearby Connections has a 32 KB limit on single payloads. Bumblebee's proprietary Nectar engine slices 1280px photos into 18KB chunk packets and reassembles them smoothly on the receiving device with zero visual degradation.

Where is my data stored, and how is local privacy protected?

All chat history, voice notes, and media are saved strictly on your local handset in Android's private app sandbox (/data/data/com.bumblebee.chat/databases/ via Room SQLite), shielded by OS-level Linux UID boundaries. There are zero cloud servers, zero analytics SDKs, and zero crash trackers.

How does 4-digit mutual authentication prevent eavesdropping?

During initial connection negotiation, devices execute a UKEY2 Elliptic-Curve Diffie-Hellman handshake. Both phones calculate and display a matching 4-digit Short Authentication String (SAS) token. Users visually confirm this PIN before opening the radio tunnel, preventing Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) or rogue connection attempts.

What information is shared during an Emergency SOS broadcast?

When you explicitly trigger an Emergency SOS Buzz, Bumblebee reads your device's last known hardware GPS satellite fix and current battery percentage. This telemetry is transmitted over the air to all listening peers in range to allow rapid search-and-rescue localization without cellular towers.

Is Bumblebee completely open-source?

Yes! Bumblebee is completely open-source under the permissive MIT License. You can review the code, verify the radio packet protocols, build the project from source with Gradle, or contribute on GitHub.
